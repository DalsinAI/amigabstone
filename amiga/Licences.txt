The game data is NOT included.

- The shareware episode of Aliens of Gold (1bs30.zip, version 3.0:

  SANIM.BS1, VGADICT.BS1, VGAGRAPH.BS1, VGAHEAD.BS1, VSWAP.BS1) is
  Apogee's. Its VENDOR.DOC lets anyone pass the shareware around free of
  charge, but only as the whole, unmodified program with all its files
  (BS_AOG.EXE and the rest) "on IBM compatible media"; an Amiga drawer
  holding only the data files is not that. So the port does not ship it:
  get the shareware archive and copy its data files into the drawer.
- The full games (Aliens of Gold, .BS6; Planet Strike, .VSI) are sold by
  Apogee (3D Realms), GOG and Steam; bring your own copy.
